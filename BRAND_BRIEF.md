# Personal Brand Brief — Sanatan Roy

Based on your profile, PRD, and design requirements (mapped across 5 core positioning areas):

### 1. COLOUR DIRECTION
- **Description:** A light-first canvas built on soft off-white backgrounds (`#FAFAFA`) paired with crisp white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) and calm neutral slate-gray text (`#18181B` / `#64748B`). Deep indigo (`#4F46E5`) serves as the primary anchor color for buttons and primary navigation elements, keeping the palette focused, readable, and grounded without feeling corporate or cold.  
  *(Traceable to Answer 2: Visual Style & Aesthetics)*
- **Accent & Purpose:** Emerald Green (`#10B981`) is used as the single operational accent color exclusively to highlight live activity indicators (such as the status badge "Open to learning & opportunities") and success states on forms, clearly signaling availability and progress without visual clutter.  
  *(Traceable to Answer 2: Visual Style & Aesthetics & Answer 5: Constraints & Exclusions)*

---

### 2. TYPOGRAPHY
- **Heading Style:** Medium-to-bold weight sans-serif headings using rounded, highly readable typefaces (Inter or Manrope style) with comfortable letter-spacing. Headings are structured with clear size hierarchy (56–64px for Hero, 32–38px for section headers) to feel approachable, clear, and human rather than heavy or aggressive.  
  *(Traceable to Answer 3: Typography & Readability)*
- **Body Style:** Regular weight sans-serif body text (16–18px) set with open line-height (1.6) and capped line-lengths for comfortable, effortless reading across desktop and mobile devices.  
  *(Traceable to Answer 3: Typography & Readability)*

---

### 3. TONE OF VOICE
- **How the writing on the site should sound:** Grounded, honest, curious, and direct. The copy presents a student developer who is actively experimenting and building real projects, avoiding exaggerated job titles, marketing buzzwords, or inflated claims of expertise.  
  *(Traceable to Answer 1: Identity & Positioning)*
- **Example Sentence:** “Hi, I'm Sanatan 👋 — a CSE (AI & ML) student at GIET University building practical software, experimenting with AI, and turning ideas into functional projects.”  
  *(Traceable to Answer 1: Identity & Positioning)*

---

### 4. DENSITY
- **Airy or Compact:** Airy.  
  *(Traceable to Answer 4: Spacing & Layout Density)*
- **What that means for spacing:** Generous vertical section padding (90–120px on desktop, 65–80px on mobile) with wide margins around project cards (`max-width: 1200px`). This creates substantial whitespace so each technical project stands out as an individual focal point without visual competition.  
  *(Traceable to Answer 4: Spacing & Layout Density)*

---

### 5. AVOID
- **Explicit list of things this brand should never do:**
  1. Never display arbitrary skill percentage bars (e.g., “Python 90%”). *(Traceable to Answer 5)*
  2. Never describe Python or software skills as “expert” or “advanced” (reflect beginner-to-intermediate experience). *(Traceable to Answer 1 & 5)*
  3. Never use neon cyberpunk aesthetics, dark glowing grids, gaming visuals, or constant background animations. *(Traceable to Answer 2 & 5)*
  4. Never present architectural blueprints or prototypes as deployed production systems. *(Traceable to Answer 1 & 5)*
  5. Never publicly display phone numbers or sensitive academic marksheet documents. *(Traceable to Answer 5)*
  6. Never display participation certificates as professional technical certifications. *(Traceable to Answer 5)*
